# Basic Image Navigation

Mirador utilizes [OpenSeadragon](https://openseadragon.github.io/) to provide deep-zoom capabilities across high-resolution IIIF images. Whether inspecting medieval calligraphy, illuminated initials, or multi-page bound codices, Mirador gives you full control over how canvases are displayed and navigated.

In this guide, we explore the 15th-century Middle English manuscript *Mandeville's Travels* ([NLS MS Adv.MS.19.1.11](https://view.nls.uk/manifest/1334/7515/133475158/manifest.json)), with special attention to **Pages 8 and 9**.

![Mirador Single Canvas View - Page 8 of Mandeville's Travels](/img/user-guide/nls-manifest-single-view.png)

## Zoom, Pan, and Inspect Details

### Zooming into Manuscript Script

You can adjust the zoom level using multiple interaction methods:

- **Floating Zoom Controls**: Use the overlay controls positioned on the right side of the canvas:
  - **`+` (Zoom in)**: Increases magnification smoothly to inspect intricate penstrokes and marginalia.
  - **`-` (Zoom out)**: Decreases magnification.
  - **`⌂` (Reset zoom)**: Resets the canvas to fit perfectly within the window bounds.
- **Mouse Wheel / Trackpad**: Scroll up or down to zoom continuously towards the cursor position.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**:
  - `+` or `=` to zoom in.
  - `-` to zoom out.
  - `0` to reset zoom to default.
- **Double-Click**: Double-click on any word or illumination to zoom in directly onto that region.

![Deep-Zoom Detail of 15th-Century Middle English Text on Page 8](/img/user-guide/nls-canvas-zoomed.png)

### Panning

To explore different folios or margins while zoomed in:

- **Click and Drag**: Click anywhere on the canvas and drag to pan across the manuscript leaf.
- **Arrow Keys**: Use `Up`, `Down`, `Left`, and `Right` arrow keys to pan incrementally.

### Image Rotation & Manifest-Declared Rotation

Users can rotate canvases interactively when rotation controls or keyboard commands are enabled:

> [!NOTE]
> **Manifest-Declared Rotation ([IIIF Recipe 0040](https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/recipe/0040-image-rotation-service/))**:
> The IIIF specification allows manifests to request initial image rotation (via `ImageApiSelector` rotation or CSS stylesheets). Mirador ignores these manifest-level rotation directives; users can rotate images manually via viewer controls, but a manifest cannot force initial rotation.

## Window View Modes

Mirador supports three distinct viewing modes tailored to different types of reading and research workflows:

![Window Views and Thumbnail Placement Menu](/img/user-guide/nls-window-views-menu.png)

To switch view modes, click the **Window views & thumbnail display** button (`⊞`) in the top-right toolbar of the window:

### 1. Single View (Focused Reading)

**Single View** (default) renders one canvas at a time centered within the viewport. It is ideal for close paleographic analysis of a single folio, such as Page 8 or Page 9:

![Single View of Page 9](/img/user-guide/nls-page9-single-view.png)

### 2. Book View (Pages 8 and 9 Facing Spread)

**Book View** renders adjacent canvases side by side as open spreads, recreating the physical experience of reading the bound medieval manuscript. Pages 8 and 9 form a stunning two-page opening:

![Book View - Pages 8 and 9 Facing Spread](/img/user-guide/nls-pages8-9-book-view.png)

- Mirador automatically preserves page sequencing and rectos/versos.
- When navigating forward or backward, two pages turn simultaneously.

> [!NOTE]
> **Book View Behavior**: In Book view, pan and zoom positions reset when turning pages, and centering may adjust slightly to accommodate differing canvas aspect ratios. Book view appears as an option in the menu only when the publishing institution designates the item as paged in its manifest (`behavior: ["paged"]` in v3 or `viewingHint: "paged"` in v2).

#### Viewing Direction Support
Mirador supports all four standardized IIIF `viewingDirection` values:
- `left-to-right`: Default Western reading order.
- `right-to-left`: Used for Hebrew, Arabic, Persian, and traditional East Asian bindings. Mirador flips the spread order and pagination controls.
- `top-to-bottom` & `bottom-to-top`: Supported for vertical scrolls and accordion codices.

### 3. Gallery View (Thumbnail Grid)

**Gallery View** transforms the window into a visual grid of all 370 canvases in the manuscript:

![Gallery View - Grid of Folio Thumbnails](/img/user-guide/nls-gallery-view.png)

- Use Gallery view to scan rapidly through an entire volume to find illuminations, chapter dividers, or specific folios.
- Click any thumbnail in the gallery (such as Page 8 or 9) to immediately open that canvas in Single view.

## Thumbnail Filmstrip Navigation

For continuous reading without sacrificing canvas space, Mirador allows you to toggle a persistent thumbnail filmstrip along the bottom of the window:

![Bottom Thumbnail Filmstrip Showing Pages 8 and 9](/img/user-guide/nls-thumbnail-strip-bottom.png)

### Thumbnail Placement Options

In the **Window views & thumbnail display** menu (`⊞`), you can configure thumbnail placement:

- **Off**: Hides thumbnails to maximize available screen area for the canvas.
- **Bottom**: Displays a horizontal scrolling filmstrip along the bottom edge of the window.
- **Right**: Displays a vertical scrolling thumbnail bar along the right edge of the window.

### Stepping Between Canvases

At the bottom of the canvas viewport, the canvas navigation bar provides:

- **Previous item (`<`)**: Steps backward to the previous canvas (e.g., from Page 9 back to Page 8).
- **Next item (`>`)**: Steps forward to the next canvas (e.g., from Page 8 to Page 9).
- **Canvas Indicator (`8 of 370 • 8`)**: Displays the current position and total canvas count within the active manuscript sequence.

## Optional Window Tools

Institutions frequently extend Mirador with community toolbar plugins. If enabled by your institution, these appear in the window header or directly over the canvas:

- **Image Tools (`mirador-image-tools`)**: An interactive toolbar positioned across the top of the canvas offering controls to rotate (in 90° increments), horizontally/vertically flip, invert colors, and fine-tune image brightness, contrast, and saturation sliders. Click `✕` to collapse the toolbar, or toggle its visibility from the window top-bar menu.
- **Share (`mirador-share-plugin`)**: Opens a modal dialog providing a direct link to the item, an HTML `<iframe>` embed snippet with configurable dimensions, a draggable IIIF badge to drop the object into other IIIF tools, and a copy button for the raw manifest URI.
- **Download (`mirador-download-plugin`)**: Opens a download dialog offering multiple image export sizes (full resolution, large, medium, thumbnail) and associated artifacts published with the manifest (such as PDF representations or OCR plain text).

## Quick Reference: Navigation Shortcuts

| Action | Control / Shortcut |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Zoom In** | `+` / Scroll Up / Double-click |
| **Zoom Out** | `-` / Scroll Down |
| **Reset Zoom** | `0` or Home button (`⌂`) |
| **Pan** | Click + Drag / Arrow keys |
| **Next Canvas** | Right Arrow (`>`) |
| **Previous Canvas** | Left Arrow (`<`) |
| **Toggle Sidebar** | Click `☰` in window header |
| **Switch Views** | Click `⊞` > Single / Book / Gallery |
