# Basic Image Navigation

Mirador utilizes [OpenSeadragon](https://openseadragon.github.io/) to provide deep-zoom capabilities across high-resolution IIIF images. Whether inspecting gigapixel art scans, delicate manuscript handwriting, or multi-page books, Mirador gives you full control over how canvases are displayed and navigated.

![Mirador Single Canvas View](/img/user-guide/manifest-single-view.png)

---

## Zoom, Pan, and Inspect Details

### Zooming

You can adjust the zoom level using multiple interaction methods:

- **Floating Zoom Controls**: Use the overlay controls positioned in the canvas viewport:
  - **`+` (Zoom in)**: Increases magnification smoothly.
  - **`-` (Zoom out)**: Decreases magnification.
  - **`⌂` (Reset zoom)**: Resets the canvas to fit perfectly within the window bounds.
- **Mouse Wheel / Trackpad**: Scroll up or down to zoom continuously towards the cursor position.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**:
  - `+` or `=` to zoom in.
  - `-` to zoom out.
  - `0` to reset zoom to default.
- **Double-Click**: Double-click on any part of the canvas to zoom in directly onto that region.

![Zoomed-in Detail Inspection](/img/user-guide/canvas-zoomed.png)

### Panning

To explore different areas of a high-resolution canvas while zoomed in:

- **Click and Drag**: Click anywhere on the canvas and drag to pan across the image.
- **Arrow Keys**: Use `Up`, `Down`, `Left`, and `Right` arrow keys to pan incrementally.

## Window View Modes

Mirador supports three distinct viewing modes tailored to different types of reading and research workflows:

![Window Views and Thumbnail Menu](/img/user-guide/window-views-menu.png)

To switch view modes, click the **Window views & thumbnail display** button (`⊞`) in the top-right toolbar of the window:

### 1. Single View

**Single View** (default) renders one canvas at a time centered within the viewport. It is ideal for close visual analysis of individual artworks, maps, photographs, and focused manuscript transcription.

### 2. Book View (Two-Up Facing Pages)

**Book View** renders adjacent canvases side by side as open spreads, mimicking the physical experience of reading a printed book or bound codex.

![Book View - Two-Up Facing Pages](/img/user-guide/book-view.png)

- Mirador automatically accounts for page sequencing and cover pages.
- When navigating forward or backward, two pages turn simultaneously.
- If the manifest declares a `viewingDirection` of `right-to-left` (common in Hebrew, Arabic, and East Asian texts), Mirador arranges the facing pages and navigation direction accordingly.

### 3. Gallery View

**Gallery View** transforms the window into a visual grid of all canvases in the manifest:

![Gallery View - Thumbnail Grid](/img/user-guide/gallery-view.png)

- Use Gallery view to scan rapidly through an entire book or manuscript to locate illustrations, blank pages, or specific sections.
- Click any thumbnail in the gallery to immediately open that canvas in Single view.
- A search query or filter applied in the sidebar highlights matching thumbnails across the gallery.

## Thumbnail Navigation

For continuous reading without sacrificing canvas space, Mirador allows you to toggle a persistent thumbnail filmstrip:

![Bottom Thumbnail Filmstrip](/img/user-guide/thumbnail-strip-bottom.png)

### Thumbnail Placement Options

In the **Window views & thumbnail display** menu, you can configure thumbnail placement:

- **Off**: Hides thumbnails to maximize available screen area for the canvas.
- **Bottom**: Displays a horizontal scrolling filmstrip along the bottom edge of the window.
- **Right**: Displays a vertical scrolling thumbnail bar along the right edge of the window.

### Stepping Between Canvases

At the bottom of the canvas viewport, the canvas navigation bar provides:

- **Previous item (`<`)**: Steps backward to the previous canvas.
- **Next item (`>`)**: Steps forward to the next canvas.
- **Canvas Indicator (`1 of 614`)**: Displays the current position and total canvas count within the active sequence.

## Quick Reference: Navigation Shortcuts

| Action | Control / Shortcut |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Zoom In** | `+` / Scroll Up / Double-click |
| **Zoom Out** | `-` / Scroll Down |
| **Reset Zoom** | `0` or Home button (`⌂`) |
| **Pan** | Click + Drag / Arrow keys |
| **Next Canvas** | Right Arrow (`>`) |
| **Previous Canvas** | Left Arrow (`<`) |
| **Toggle Sidebar** | Click `☰` in window header |
| **Switch Views** | Click `⊞` > Single / Book / Gallery |
