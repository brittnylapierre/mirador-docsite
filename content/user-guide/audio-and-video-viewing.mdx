# Audio and Video Viewing

With the adoption of the [IIIF Presentation API version 3.0](https://iiif.io/api/presentation/3.0/), IIIF expanded beyond static images to encompass time-based media: sound recordings, oral histories, musical performances, historical film, and video.

Mirador natively supports time-based A/V canvases, presenting standard playback controls and synchronized captions within its multi-window workspace.

---

## Playing Time-Based Media

When you open a manifest containing audio or video canvases:

- Mirador replaces the static OpenSeadragon deep-zoom canvas with a native HTML5 media player viewport.
- Standard media controls are presented:
  - **Play / Pause**: Start or stop playback.
  - **Seek Bar / Scrubber**: Drag the timeline slider to jump to any point in the recording.
  - **Time Elapsed / Total Duration**: Displays current timestamp and total track length.
  - **Volume & Mute**: Adjust output sound level or silence audio.
  - **Fullscreen (Video)**: Expand video playback to fill the display.

> [!NOTE]
> Mirador relies on the host browser's native `<video>` and `<audio>` elements. Media files must be encoded in browser-compatible formats such as MP4 (H.264/AAC), WebM (VP8/VP9/Opus), MP3, AAC, or WAV. Publishers can declare multiple format options; Mirador renders these as `<source>` elements, allowing your browser to select the first codec it supports. Adaptive bitrate streaming protocols like HLS (`.m3u8`) or MPEG-DASH (`.mpd`) are not supported out of the box and require custom video player plugins.

> **The Architectural Pattern:**
> Mirador handles A/V *playback* reliably, but not A/V *time-based structure*. If your manifests depend on timestamps—such as segmented oral histories, timed annotations, or automated playlists—Mirador currently will not honor them.

---

## Captions and Subtitles (WebVTT)

Mirador supports synchronized captions and subtitles declared as supplementing annotations:

- When a video manifest includes a WebVTT file (`text/vtt`) attached as an annotation with `"motivation": "supplementing"`, Mirador displays the native subtitle toggle.
- Captions synchronize automatically with video playback using the browser's built-in text track engine.
- Multiple subtitle tracks (e.g., different languages or descriptions for the hearing impaired) can be selected via the player controls.

### Plain Text & HTML Transcripts

> [!WARNING]
> While WebVTT (`text/vtt`) supplementing annotations are fully supported, full text or HTML transcripts supplied as `text/plain` or `text/html` supplementing annotations are **not** currently rendered by Mirador's player panel. If your workflow relies on synchronized paragraph transcriptions, consider packaging them as WebVTT cues or using a dedicated AV viewer integration.

---

## Comparing Audio/Video with Static Objects

Because Mirador is a multi-window workspace, you can combine time-based media with static texts:

- Open a video recording of an oral history interview in Window 1.
- Open the digitized handwritten interview transcript or photographic portraits in Window 2.
- Listen to audio recordings of an orchestral performance while reading the musical manuscript score side by side.

## Known Time-Based Media Limitations

When preparing audio and video manifests for Mirador, be aware of the following specification boundaries documented in the [Mirador IIIF Support Matrix](/iiif-support):

| Capability | Status | Practical Impact | Specification Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Native HTML5 Playback** | ✅ Supported | Plays any format natively supported by your browser (MP4, WebM, MP3, WAV). | [IIIF Presentation 3.0](https://iiif.io/api/presentation/3.0/) |
| **Synchronized VTT Captions** | ✅ Supported | WebVTT supplementing annotations render as native browser subtitles. | [IIIF Cookbook](https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/) |
| **HLS / MPEG-DASH Streaming** | ❌ Not supported | Adaptive bitrate video streams cannot be played natively without custom plugins. | — |
| **Non-VTT Transcripts** | ❌ Not supported | `text/plain` and `text/html` transcripts are ignored by the UI. | — |
| **Temporal Media Fragments** | ❌ Not supported | Fragment targets (e.g. `#t=120,180` or `#t=60`) on canvases are ignored. Mirador cannot jump to specific timestamps from TOC ranges or render time-targeted annotations. | [W3C Media Fragments 1.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/media-frags/) |
| **Auto-Advance & Repeat** | ❌ Not supported | Presentation v3 playlist-style `auto-advance` and `repeat` behaviors are not handled. Tracks stop when finished. | [IIIF Presentation 3.0 Behaviors](https://iiif.io/api/presentation/3.0/#behavior) |
| **Accompanying Canvases** | ❌ Not supported | Secondary synchronized canvases (such as a sign-language video alongside a lecture) are not rendered. | [Cookbook Recipe 0014](https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/recipe/0014-accompanyingcanvas/) |
| **A/V Choice Resources** | ❌ Not supported | The Choice UI only activates for images. Alternative audio/video bitrates or camera angles declared via Choice bodies are not exposed. | — |
