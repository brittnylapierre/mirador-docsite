# Using the Side Panels and Metadata

Every viewing window in Mirador includes a collapsible **Companion Sidebar** that surfaces contextual metadata, structural indices, commentary, and rights statements embedded in the IIIF resource.

To toggle the companion sidebar, click the **Show sidebar** / **Toggle sidebar** button (`☰`) in the top-left corner of the window.

---

## 1. Information Panel

The **Information** tab (represented by the `ⓘ` icon) presents cataloging and descriptive metadata for both the overall manifest and the currently active canvas:

![Sidebar Information Panel](/img/user-guide/sidebar-info.png)

### Key Sections:

- **Manifest Title & Summary**: Displays the formal title, alternate titles, dates, and descriptive summaries provided by the publishing institution.
- **Metadata Key-Value Pairs**: Formatted list of bibliographic and archival fields, including:
  - *Author / Creator*
  - *Publication Date & Place*
  - *Publisher / Holding Institution*
  - *Physical Description & Dimensions*
  - *Classification / Call Numbers*
- **Canvas Metadata**: Specific metadata applicable only to the active page or sheet (e.g., specific folio numbers, transcription notes, or dimensions).
- **Related Links**: Direct URLs to catalog records, institutional holding pages, or alternative downloadable formats (PDF, TEI/XML).

---

## 2. Rights and Attribution Panel

The **Rights** tab (represented by the copyright/shield icon) provides legal and licensing information governing how the resource may be used, shared, and cited:

![Sidebar Rights Panel](/img/user-guide/sidebar-rights.png)

### What You Will Find:

- **Attribution & Holding Institution**: Acknowledgment of the library, archive, or museum providing the digital surrogate (e.g., *Wellcome Collection, London*).
- **Rights Statement & License**: Standardized RightsStatements.org or Creative Commons URI licenses (e.g., *Public Domain Mark 1.0*, *CC-BY 4.0*, *In Copyright*).
- **Institutional Logo**: Digital watermark or emblem representing the repository.

---

## 3. Index Panel (Table of Contents & Canvases)

The **Index** tab (represented by the book/list icon) displays the navigational structure of the resource:

![Sidebar Index Panel](/img/user-guide/sidebar-index.png)

- **Canvas List**: A scrollable vertical list of every canvas in the sequence with its page label.
- **Table of Contents (Ranges)**: When the manifest defines structural ranges, nested chapter titles appear in a collapsible hierarchy.
- Clicking any canvas or chapter immediately scrolls and navigates the main viewer to that location.

---

## 4. Annotations Panel

The **Annotations** tab (represented by the speech bubble icon) manages all annotations attached to the active canvas:

![Sidebar Annotations Panel](/img/user-guide/sidebar-annotations.png)

- Lists all annotations targeting the visible page or specific regions within it.
- Allows researchers to inspect transcription text, historical commentary, or object tags.
- Toggles visibility of annotation bounding boxes and SVG highlight polygons on the canvas.

---

## 5. Content Search Panel

The **Search** tab (represented by the magnifying glass icon) enables full-text querying across the manifest when a IIIF Content Search service is available:

![Sidebar Search Panel](/img/user-guide/sidebar-search.png)

- Input search keywords to query OCR transcripts or transcribed text layers.
- View matching text snippets with direct links to the exact canvas and coordinate regions where the terms appear.

---

## Companion Window Controls

The companion sidebar is designed to fit your screen and workflow:

- **Collapse / Expand**: Click the sidebar toggle icon (`☰`) at any time to open or hide the panel, reclaiming full window width for image viewing.
- **Separate Panel**: In multi-window or expanded layouts, sidebars can be docked or opened as separate sub-panels to keep metadata visible alongside multiple comparisons.
- **Responsive Stacking**: On narrower screens or when multiple windows are tiled, side panels automatically adjust their width and stack gracefully.
