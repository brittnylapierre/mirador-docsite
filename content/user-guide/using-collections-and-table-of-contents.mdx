# Using collections and table of contents

This page covers navigation through collections and structured resources.

## Browsing collections

- Open a collection to browse its related resources.
- Move through parent and child items to find the right object.
- A collection may be the best way to understand the broader context of a resource set.

## Understanding resource hierarchy

- A collection may contain manifests and sub-collections.
- A manifest may contain sequences and canvases.
- The structure helps users understand where an item sits within a larger object or set.

## Moving through structure

- Table-of-contents navigation is often used with books, manuscripts, and archival objects.
- Use the structure to move between sections instead of scanning page by page.
- This is especially useful for long or highly organized materials.

## Selecting a canvas from the table of contents

- Click a table-of-contents item to jump directly to the relevant canvas.
- This helps align reading order, navigation, and the object’s logical structure.
- A well-structured manifest can make the viewer easier to use and easier to understand.
