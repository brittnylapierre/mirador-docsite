# Language, Locale, and Appearance Settings

Mirador is designed for international research communities and diverse reading environments. Users can easily customize the interface appearance, toggle dark mode, switch between dozens of localized languages, and view materials formatted for right-to-left reading directions.

All workspace-wide settings are accessible from the **Workspace settings** icon (`⚙` / palette) located in the top workspace control panel:

![Workspace Settings Menu](/img/user-guide/nls-workspace-settings-menu.png)

---

## Dark Theme and Light Theme

Mirador provides high-contrast, accessibility-tested themes:

- **Light theme** (default): Clean, white background ideal for brightly lit environments and standard reading.
- **Dark theme**: Deep-slate dark background designed to reduce eye strain in low-light environments, photographic darkrooms, and conservation studios. Notice how manuscript folios (such as Pages 8 and 9) stand out prominently against the dark backdrop:

![Workspace Dark Theme Showing Pages 8 and 9](/img/user-guide/nls-workspace-theme-dark.png)

### How to Change the Theme:

1. Click the **Workspace settings** icon (`⚙`) in the top workspace control panel.
2. Hover over or click **Change theme**.
3. Select **Dark theme** or **Light theme**. The entire UI—including window headers, menus, sidebars, and dialogs—updates instantly.

- Italiano (`it`)

- 中文 (`zh-CN` / `zh-TW`)
- Nederlands (`nl`)
- Português (`pt-BR`)
- עברית (`he`)
- العربية (`ar`)
- ...and many others.

### How to Switch Language:

1. Click the **Workspace settings** icon (`⚙`).
2. Click **Language**.
3. Choose your preferred language from the list. All UI labels, tooltips, dialogs, and button descriptions will update immediately.

### Two Interacting Language Systems

Mirador coordinates two distinct language systems:

- **App Language**: The language of Mirador's menus, buttons, companion headers, and dialogs.
- **Manifest Language**: The language of the digitized item's titles, summaries, metadata pairs, and annotations, as authored by the holding institution.

If a manifest provides descriptive metadata in multiple languages, a language dropdown appears inside the **Information** panel (`ⓘ`). Choosing an app language that the manifest also offers will automatically select that language in the manifest metadata dropdown.

> [!NOTE]
> State alignment between app language and manifest metadata language can occasionally experience stale-state synchronization in complex workspaces. You can always manually choose your preferred metadata language using the dropdown in the Information companion sidebar.

## Toggling Zoom Controls

For presentations or uncluttered visual viewing:

1. Click the **Workspace settings** icon (`⚙`).
2. Click **Hide zoom controls** (or **Show zoom controls**).
3. The floating zoom and pan buttons on the canvas viewport will toggle on or off. Even with controls hidden, you can continue zooming and panning using mouse wheel, trackpad, or keyboard shortcuts.

---

## Right-to-Left (RTL) Reading & Interface Support

Mirador provides comprehensive right-to-left support, maintaining a strict distinction between **interface layout** and **content viewing direction**:

- **UI Layout Direction**: When you select an RTL interface language (such as Arabic or Hebrew), Mirador flips the entire Material UI interface—the workspace control bar, window sidebars, toolbars, and dialog layouts invert horizontally to follow RTL conventions.
- **Viewing Direction (Independent of UI Language)**: A manifest's declared `viewingDirection` (`right-to-left`) controls page navigation arrows, thumbnail filmstrip ordering, and facing-page layouts in Book View. A researcher viewing a Hebrew manuscript within an English-language UI will still navigate pages right-to-left with correct recto/verso page spreads.

## Optional Toolbar Tools

Depending on how your institution configures Mirador, additional toolbars and modal dialogs may be available in the window:

- **Image Tools Toolbar (`mirador-image-tools`)**: A floating horizontal toolbar above the canvas providing interactive tools to rotate (90° steps), flip horizontally/vertically, and fine-tune brightness, contrast, and saturation sliders in real time. Can be collapsed with the `✕` icon or hidden via the window menu.
- **Share Dialog (`mirador-share-plugin`)**: Surfaces a shareable link, responsive `<iframe>` embed snippet, draggable IIIF badge, and a copy button for the raw manifest URI.
- **Download Modal (`mirador-download-plugin`)**: Enables downloading high-resolution image crops or entire rendered artifacts (such as OCR text files or PDFs).
