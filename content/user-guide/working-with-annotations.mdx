# Working with Annotations

In the IIIF ecosystem, annotations are first-class citizens. Annotations link commentary, scholarly transcriptions, translations, visual highlights, and tags to specific canvases or spatial coordinate regions (`xywh` or SVG polygons).

Mirador renders annotations seamlessly over the image canvas while providing a structured list in the companion sidebar.

![Sidebar Annotations Panel](/img/user-guide/sidebar-annotations.png)

---

## Viewing Canvas Annotations

When a loaded manifest includes linked `AnnotationList` (Presentation v2) or `AnnotationPage` (Presentation v3) resources:

1. Click the **Toggle sidebar** button (`☰`) in the window header.
2. Select the **Annotations** tab (speech bubble icon).
3. The Annotations panel lists all annotations associated with the active canvas.

---

## Anatomy of an Annotation in Mirador

Mirador supports standard W3C Web Annotation and Open Annotation data models:

- **Target / Spatial Selector**: A rectangular fragment (`#xywh=100,200,300,150`) or complex SVG path defining where the annotation appears on the canvas.
- **Body / Content**: The textual transcription, translation, explanatory comment, or HTML-formatted note.
- **Motivation**: Identifies the purpose of the annotation (e.g., `commenting`, `supplementing`, `tagging`, `describing`).
- **Creator / Attribution**: Metadata identifying who created the annotation and when it was authored.

---

## Interacting with Annotations on the Canvas

- **Canvas Overlays**: Bounding boxes or shaded SVG polygons are drawn directly over the image canvas.
- **Hover & Tooltip**: Hovering your cursor over an annotated region highlights the corresponding text in the sidebar.
- **Click to Focus**: Clicking an annotation in the sidebar zooms the canvas directly to that region and displays the commentary card.
- **Toggle Visibility**: Use the visibility toggle icon in the Annotations panel header to hide or show annotation bounding boxes on the canvas, allowing unobstructed inspection of the underlying image.

---

## Annotation Collections & External Annotation Servers

Institutions often host annotations on external annotation servers (such as Elucidate or custom backends) or link them via paginated `AnnotationCollection` records.

> [!NOTE]
> Mirador renders and navigates pre-existing annotations published with manifests. For in-viewer annotation creation, drawing tools, and authoring workflows, community plugins (such as `mirador-annotations`) can be integrated. Consult the [Plugins Guide](/install-config/plugins) for implementation details.
