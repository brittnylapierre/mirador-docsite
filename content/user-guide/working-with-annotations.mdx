# Working with annotations

Mirador can display annotations when the IIIF resource includes annotation content or an annotation service.

## How to view annotations

- Open the annotations panel or overlay controls to review visible annotation sets.
- Use the viewer to see annotation markup over the image or within the resource context.
- Check which annotation list is active before reviewing the content.

## Understanding annotation types and motivations

- Textual annotations may explain or describe part of the image.
- Region-based annotations may highlight a specific area on the canvas.
- Annotation motivations vary, such as commenting, bookmarking, or identifying key features.

## Filtering annotation sets

- Reduce clutter by filtering annotation sets or layers.
- Focus on the annotation source most relevant to the current task.
- This helps when a single object includes multiple annotation providers or review groups.

## Reading annotation content and metadata

- Review the text body, labels, and metadata attached to each annotation.
- Consider source, author, date, and related resource information when interpreting what is shown.

## How annotations appear over the image

- Annotations may appear as overlays, regions, markers, or linked notes.
- They often correspond to a specific canvas area and help draw attention to notable details.
- Use the annotation layer to understand both the image and the commentary attached to it.
